03 / 05 Oda Nobunaga Introduction 03 / 05

Oda Nobunaga is known as the first great unifier of Japan - a daimyō who overthrew the Ashikaga shogunate and ended years of feudal wars in Japan

Accounts describe him as tall, lean and lightly bearded. His ambition made him ruthless, yet he was a clear-eyed realist, respected for making the hard calls.
